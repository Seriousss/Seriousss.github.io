Hi, I'm a first-year Ph.D. Student at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), affiliated with the [Language Technologies Institute](https://lti.cs.cmu.edu/) in the [School of Computer Science](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/). I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Sean Welleck](https://wellecks.com/).

I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/). During my undergraduate studies, I had the opportunity to work with Prof. [Reyhaneh Jabbarvand](https://reyhaneh.cs.illinois.edu/) (UIUC), Prof. [Xiaodong Gu](https://guxd.github.io/) (SJTU), Prof. [Xinyu Wang](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~xwangsd/) (University of Michigan), and Prof. [Alex Aiken](https://theory.stanford.edu/~aiken/) (Stanford).

My research interests lie broadly in machine learning, spanning agentic systems and model training. I also have a background in programming languages and software engineering. My recent work mainly focuses on two directions:

(1) Building **scalable agentic systems** for challenging real-world tasks from software engineering to scientific discovery. [[Rustine]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20617) [[Astra]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07506)

(2) Advancing models’ **formal reasoning capabilities** to synthesize verifiably correct programs and proofs, with the goal of improving the trustworthiness and safety of systems as coding agents become increasingly widely adopted. [[Quokka]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21629) [[PoPilot]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11901)

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to chat about research or potential collaborations! I'm also looking for internship opportunities🙌🏻
